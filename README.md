# MPRD-Ecosystem

Research ecosystem developed by Martin Pitre.

---

## Overview

This repository contains a collection of exploratory research frameworks, algorithms, validation systems, uncertainty analysis tools, and evidence archives developed as part of an ongoing research program focused on uncertainty, learning, recursive development, and evidence accumulation.

---

## Repository Structure

### MPRD

Martin Pitre Framework of Relational Distinction, Persistence, and Recursive Development.

Foundational theoretical framework describing emergence, persistence, learning, self-modeling, self-knowing, truth integration, and recursive development.

---

### SUANR_V1

Stochastic Uncertainty-Aware Neural Regression.

Machine learning research project focused on uncertainty-aware prediction and confidence estimation.

Publication Version: SUANR v1

---

### UTA

Uncertainty Transformation Analysis.

Research framework designed to measure and classify how uncertainty changes through events, discoveries, technologies, and historical developments.

---

### UTU

Uncertainty Targeting Unit.

Research scouting system designed to identify observations, anomalies, questions, and potential research opportunities.

---

### Horizon

Detection and Validation System.

Experimental framework for signal detection, recovery validation, robustness testing, and statistical verification.

---

### UBv1

Research Memory, Evidence Ledger, and Knowledge Archive.

Stores observations, evidence, test results, recommendations, and research history.
