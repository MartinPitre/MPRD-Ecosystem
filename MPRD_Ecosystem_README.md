## Overview

The MPRD Ecosystem is an independent research initiative exploring uncertainty, observation, validation, prediction, and evidence-based knowledge development.

Rather than functioning as a single project, MPRD consists of a collection of interconnected research architectures, frameworks, methodologies, and supporting systems.

## Ecosystem Architecture

MPRD Ecosystem

- UTU_V1 (Observation Scout)
- PBO_V1 (Observation Patrol)
- AIRA_V1 (Relationship Assessor)
- UTA_V1 (Uncertainty Analyzer)
- UB_V1 (Knowledge Ledger)
- SUANR_V1 (Uncertainty-Aware Prediction)
- Horizon_V1 (Validation Framework)
- MKCS (Future Adaptive Coordination System)

## Recursive Research Flow

Observation
→ UTU / PBO
→ AIRA
→ UTA
→ UB
→ SUANR
→ Horizon
→ New Observations
