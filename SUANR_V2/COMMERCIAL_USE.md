SUANR_V2 is publicly available for limited non-commercial research, study,
evaluation, testing, and educational use under the terms of the included
`LICENSE`.

## Commercial use is not automatically granted

**You must obtain prior written permission from Martin Pitre before making
commercial use of SUANR_V2.**

Notification after commercial use has started is not sufficient.

Examples that normally require separate permission include:

- incorporating SUANR_V2 into a paid product or service;
- using SUANR_V2 in commercial software or a hosted API;
- using it to materially support paid consulting or contracting;
- integrating it into a revenue-generating machine-learning system;
- using it internally as part of commercial product development or service
  delivery;
- selling, sublicensing, or monetizing SUANR_V2 or a derivative implementation.

This list is illustrative rather than exhaustive. The `LICENSE` contains the
controlling definition and terms.

## Commercial licensing inquiry

A commercial-use request should identify:

1. the person or organization requesting permission;
2. the intended application;
3. whether SUANR_V2 will be modified or incorporated into another system;
4. whether the resulting system will be distributed, hosted, sold, or licensed;
5. the expected commercial context; and
6. a contact person for licensing correspondence.

Use the contact method published by Martin Pitre in the repository or associated
project profile.

## Attribution remains required

A commercial license or written permission may establish additional or modified
terms. Unless explicitly waived in writing, attribution to **Martin Pitre —
Creator of SUANR_V2** remains required.

Commercial rights exist only when granted explicitly in writing by the
