# MPRD_V1

## Martin Pitre Framework of Relational Distinction, Persistence, and Recursive Development

**Author:** Martin Pitre

**Status:** Frozen research baseline  
**Phase-1 validation:** 5 of 5 tests completed  
**Research program:** Multi-Knowledge & Cognitive System (MKCS)

## Overview

MPRD_V1 is an experimental framework for studying how a system can form distinctions, select relationships, reinforce recurring evidence, preserve sufficiently supported patterns, integrate new information, learn, and update its internal model over repeated cycles.

MPRD is the recursive foundation of the wider MKCS research program. It is intended to support transparent, measurable, and auditable experimentation—not to serve as a claim of general intelligence, consciousness, autonomous agency, or production readiness.

The frozen MPRD_V1 baseline is preserved so later experiments and staged integrations can be compared against a stable reference implementation.

## Core Research Question

> Can a recursive evidence process distinguish, reinforce, preserve, integrate, and revise relationships in a measurable and repeatable way?

## Recursive Process

MPRD_V1 follows ten connected stages:

1. **Distinction** — identify a candidate item, signal, or difference.
2. **Relational Selection** — evaluate relevant relationships among available information.
3. **Recursive Reinforcement** — strengthen relationships that receive recurring support.
4. **Persistence** — preserve relationships that meet the required support conditions.
5. **Integration** — incorporate persistent relationships into the active knowledge state.
6. **Internal Relevance** — estimate how the integrated information relates to the system's current state.
7. **Learning** — modify the working state in response to supported evidence.
8. **Self-Model Update** — revise the system's internal representation of what it currently holds.
9. **Truth Integration** — incorporate sufficiently supported information while retaining contradiction and uncertainty signals.
10. **Recursive Development** — repeat the process as additional evidence becomes available.

These stages describe an experimental computational process. Terms such as *self-model* and *truth integration* refer to internal data structures and update operations; they do not imply subjective awareness or infallible truth determination.

## Evidence Governance

This repository follows the MKCS evidence-governance approach.

### Evidence Preservation Principle

- Preserve original evidence and experimental outputs.
- Do not silently overwrite or cosmetically alter source evidence.
- Annotate only what is visibly or computationally supported.
- Label unknowns, uncertainty, corrections, and limitations explicitly.
- Keep transformations, overlays, summaries, and derived outputs traceable to their sources.

### Evidence Layer Separation

Materials should remain distinguishable across three layers:

1. **Evidence** — original inputs, code, configurations, run outputs, hashes, and measurements.
2. **Investigation** — comparisons, diagnostics, calculations, and working interpretations.
3. **Presentation** — summaries, figures, explanatory documents, and publication material.

Presentation material must not replace or modify the underlying evidence record.

## Reproducibility

For each validation:

1. Use the exact code and configuration included with that test.
2. Record the software environment, dependencies, seed values, and execution conditions when available.
3. Run the test without changing the frozen baseline.
4. Compare generated outputs with the preserved expected results.
5. Retain new outputs separately from the originals.
6. Document any mismatch, warning, correction, or environmental difference.

A successful local rerun is evidence of reproducibility under the documented conditions; it is not equivalent to independent scientific replication.

## Interpretation Boundaries

The Phase-1 results support the conclusion that MPRD_V1 met its predefined criteria within the documented controlled tests. They do not establish:

- universal correctness or scientific proof;
- generalization to every dataset or environment;
- superiority over other methods;
- safe autonomous operation;
- consciousness, sentience, or subjective understanding;
- production readiness;
- independent peer review or replication;
- validation of later MPRD_R integrations or the complete MKCS system.

Observation, interpretation, and broader hypothesis must remain clearly separated.

## Relationship to MPRD_R and MKCS

MPRD_V1 is the preserved recursive foundation used in the staged MPRD_R integration series:

- **MPRD_R_V1:** MPRD_V1 + SUANR_V2
- **MPRD_R_V2:** MPRD_R_V1 + UTU_V2
- later MPRD_R stages progressively introduce additional validated components.

Once all intended components are integrated and the complete architecture itself is tested, the resulting system will be identified as the **MKCS system**, rather than as another MPRD_R version.

Validation of MPRD_V1 does not automatically validate any integration built on top of it. Every combined configuration requires its own evidence and validation record.

## Responsible Resourcefulness Principle

An MPRD- or MKCS-based system may recognize that it lacks sufficient information and seek additional evidence, but resourcefulness must remain bounded:

> Use any authorized, ethical, and traceable means available to gather the information required—while preserving human oversight, privacy, security, evidence provenance, and the right to stop or refuse unsafe actions.

Resourcefulness never grants permission to access private information, bypass safeguards, circumvent restrictions, conceal methods, or act outside authorized scope.
