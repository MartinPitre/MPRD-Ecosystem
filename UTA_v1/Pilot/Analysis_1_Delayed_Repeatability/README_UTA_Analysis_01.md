# UTA Analysis 01 – Delayed Repeatability

## Overview

This folder contains the materials associated with Analysis 01 of the UTA (Uncertainty Transformation Analysis) pilot study.

Analysis 01 evaluated delayed repeatability by comparing participant assessments collected during Q1 and Q2.

The objective was to determine whether participant scoring patterns remained reasonably consistent when the same events were reassessed after a delay period.

---

## Research Question

Do participants demonstrate repeatable uncertainty assessments when evaluating the same events across separated assessment periods?

---

## Data Sources

The analysis was performed using the final preserved pilot datasets:

- UTA_Q1_Pilot_Dataset.xlsx
- UTA_Q2_Pilot_Dataset_Updated_v18.xlsx

Only the final corrected datasets were used for preserved analysis results.

---

## Method Summary

Participant responses from Q1 and Q2 were matched and compared.

Statistical and graphical methods were used to evaluate agreement between assessment periods and identify the overall level of delayed repeatability within the pilot sample.

Lower variation between assessments indicates greater repeatability.

## Significance

Delayed repeatability is an important indicator of assessment consistency.

Analysis 01 represents the first empirical evaluation of whether UTA-generated assessments demonstrate stability when participants independently reassess the same events over time.
