# UTA Pilot Methodology

## Overview

This folder contains documentation describing the methodology used during the initial UTA (Uncertainty Transformation Analysis) pilot study.

The pilot study was designed to evaluate the practical application of the UTA framework through independent participant assessments conducted across two assessment periods.

This methodology documentation is intended to support transparency, reproducibility, and future expansion of the UTA research program.

---

## Pilot Objective

The primary objective of the pilot study was to examine whether uncertainty assessments demonstrated measurable stability and repeatability when participants reassessed the same events after a delay period.

The study also explored whether consistent event-type patterns emerged across participant evaluations.

---

## Study Design

The pilot consisted of two assessment phases:

### Q1 Assessment

Participants independently evaluated a predefined set of events using the UTA scoring framework.

Responses were collected without group discussion or consensus scoring.

### Q2 Assessment

Participants repeated the assessment after a delay period.

Participants completed the second assessment without access to their original responses.

This design allowed evaluation of delayed repeatability and scoring stability.

---

## Participant Process

- Participation was voluntary.
- Participants could choose whether to provide identifying information.
- Public research outputs use anonymous participant identifiers.
- Individual responses were treated as independent observations.

---

## Data Collection

Data were collected using structured scoring forms developed for the UTA pilot study.

Responses were compiled into Q1 and Q2 datasets and subsequently reviewed through audit procedures prior to final analysis.

---

## Analytical Objectives

The pilot study produced four primary analyses:

1. Delayed Repeatability
2. Event Stability Audit
3. Participant Stability
4. Event-Type Signature Consistency

These analyses collectively evaluated the behavior of the UTA framework under pilot conditions.

---

## Reproducibility

All final analyses were performed using the preserved datasets contained within the Pilot/Data/Final directory.

Corrections and revisions identified during auditing were documented and incorporated into the final preserved dataset versions.
