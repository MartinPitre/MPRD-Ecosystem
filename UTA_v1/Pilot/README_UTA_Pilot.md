# UTA Pilot Study

## Overview

This folder contains the complete documentation, datasets, analyses, figures, and audit materials associated with the first UTA (Uncertainty Transformation Analysis) pilot study.

The pilot study was designed to evaluate the practical application of the UTA framework through anonymous participant assessments conducted across two collection periods (Q1 and Q2).

The purpose of the pilot was not to validate objective truth, but rather to examine the consistency and transformation of uncertainty assessments over time.

---

## Pilot Objectives

The UTA pilot study was conducted to investigate:

- Delayed repeatability of uncertainty assessments
- Stability of event classifications over time
- Participant scoring consistency
- Event-type signature behavior
- General feasibility of the UTA framework

The study represents the first empirical evaluation of UTA v1.

---

## Study Design

### Q1 Assessment

Participants independently evaluated a series of historical and scientific events using the UTA scoring framework.

### Q2 Assessment

Participants repeated the assessment after a delay period without access to their original responses.

This design allowed evaluation of delayed repeatability and scoring stability.

## Data Integrity

Corrections, revisions, and audit notes generated during the pilot process have been preserved to maintain transparency and reproducibility.

Where corrections were required, analyses were rerun using the corrected datasets.

---

## Relationship to Theory

This pilot study serves as the first empirical evaluation of the theoretical framework contained in the Theory directory.

The pilot does not replace the theory; rather, it provides evidence regarding the behavior of the framework under practical application.
