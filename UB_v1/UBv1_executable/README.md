## Overview

UBv1 (Uncertainty Bundle v1) was developed as a research ledger and uncertainty-tracking framework intended to preserve research questions, evidence, uncertainty transformations, conclusions, and future investigation pathways.

This repository preserves the first documented executable implementation of UBv1.

The purpose of this release is historical preservation and research documentation. It records the transition of UBv1 from a conceptual framework into a functioning software prototype.

---

## Historical Context

Development lineage:

MPRD → SUANR_v1 → UTA_v1 → UBv1

- **MPRD** established the foundational framework for recursive development and knowledge integration.
- **SUANR_v1** explored uncertainty-aware prediction.
- **UTA_v1** explored uncertainty transformation through reduction, creation, splitting, and merging.
- **UBv1** was proposed as a research ledger capable of preserving these activities within a structured memory system.

---

## Purpose of the Prototype

The prototype was created to determine whether UBv1 could be represented as executable software capable of:

- Creating structured research entries
- Attaching evidence records
- Tracking uncertainty transformation
- Preserving research metadata
- Generating research recommendations
