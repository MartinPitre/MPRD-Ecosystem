# UBv1 — Uncertainty Ledger and Research Diary

## Overview

UBv1 (Uncertainty Bundle v1 — Uncertainty Ledger and Research Diary) is an ongoing research ledger created by Martin Pitre to document observations, hypotheses, conceptual models, exploratory investigations, and the development of new ideas.

UBv1 serves as the historical memory of the broader research ecosystem.

The purpose of UBv1 is not to present finalized conclusions, but to preserve the evolution of understanding as uncertainty is investigated, evidence is gathered, and concepts are refined over time.

**Current Ledger Edition:** v43  
**Status:** Active Research Project

---

## Core Principle

> The incredible becomes credible through evidence.

UBv1 operates under the principle that confidence should increase through observation, testing, analysis, and evidence accumulation rather than assumption alone.

---

## Purpose

The ledger serves several functions:

- Preserve research observations.
- Record conceptual developments.
- Document uncertainty investigations.
- Track the evolution of hypotheses.
- Maintain continuity across long-term projects.
- Support future analysis and refinement.

## Research Areas

### SUANR_v1
Stochastic Uncertainty-Aware Neural Regression

Research exploring uncertainty estimation within machine learning regression systems.

### UTA_v1
Uncertainty Transformation Analysis

A framework investigating how discoveries, inventions, scientific advances, and historical events transform uncertainty.

### UTU Concepts
Uncertainty Transformation Units

Exploratory concepts examining how uncertainty investigations may be performed by individuals, teams, and larger organizational structures.

### PBO Concepts
Path-Based Optimization

Exploratory decision frameworks focused on navigating uncertainty through evidence-based pathway selection.

### MPRD Framework
Martin Pitre Framework of Relational Distinction, Persistence, and Recursive Development.

A foundational framework exploring persistence, learning, self-modeling, self-knowing, continuity, and recursive development.
