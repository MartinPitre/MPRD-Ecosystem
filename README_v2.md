# Martin Pitre Research Repository

Exploratory research in uncertainty, observation architectures, prediction systems, and recursive development.

This repository contains a collection of independent and interconnected research projects developed by Martin Pitre.

## Research Projects

### MPRD
**Martin Pitre Framework of Relational Distinction, Persistence, and Recursive Development**

Foundational theoretical framework exploring persistence, learning, recursive development, and self-knowing systems.

### SUANR_V1
**Stochastic Uncertainty-Aware Neural Regression**

Published baseline implementation for uncertainty-aware regression and predictive uncertainty estimation.

### UTA
**Uncertainty Transformation Analysis**

Research framework investigating how events transform uncertainty through reduction, creation, splitting, and merging.

### UTU
**Uncertainty Targeting Unit**

Observation and scouting architecture designed to identify anomalies, patterns, and candidate research questions.

### PBO_v1
**Patrol-Based Observation**

Multi-observer observation architecture intended to compare, aggregate, and validate observations across multiple independent observers.

### Horizon
**Signal Detection and Validation Framework**

Designed to distinguish meaningful signals from noise and support evidence-based validation of observations.

### UBv1
**Uncertainty Bundle v1**

Research ledger, evidence archive, uncertainty tracking system, and long-term knowledge repository for the research ecosystem.
