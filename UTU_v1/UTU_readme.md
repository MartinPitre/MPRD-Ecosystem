## Abstract

UTU (Uncertainty Targeting Unit) is an exploratory research framework for identifying, prioritizing, and investigating uncertainty within complex information environments. Rather than focusing solely on generating answers, UTU explores methods for determining which unanswered questions may provide the greatest informational value when investigated.

---

## Project Origin

UTU emerged from ongoing research into uncertainty, knowledge formation, evidence acquisition, and recursive development conducted through the MPRD, SUANR, UTA, and UBv1 research projects.

> How should a system decide which uncertainties are most important to investigate next?

UTU was created as an exploratory attempt to address that question through a structured uncertainty-targeting framework.

## Validation Disclaimer

UTU v1 should be interpreted as an exploratory research framework and not as a validated scientific model.

UTU is not:

- A completed scientific theory.

## Research Lineage

UTU emerged from ongoing exploratory research conducted through the following projects:

- MPRD (Martin Pitre Framework of Relational Distinction, Persistence, and Recursive Development)
- SUANR (Stochastic Uncertainty-Aware Neural Regression)
- UTA (Uncertainty Transformation Analysis)
- UBv1 (Uncertainty Bundle Research Diary)

These projects collectively investigate uncertainty, evidence acquisition, learning, recursive development, and knowledge-state evolution.

UTU represents a focused exploration into identifying, prioritizing, and investigating uncertainty itself as a potential target for systematic analysis.
