## Overview

Horizon v1 is the validation component of the MPRD Ecosystem.

Its purpose is to independently evaluate the reliability, consistency, stability, and performance of ecosystem components through structured testing and verification procedures.

While other components focus on observation, analysis, prediction, and preservation, Horizon focuses on answering a critical question:

> *How confident should we be in the results?*

---

## Purpose

Horizon v1 was created to provide a dedicated validation layer capable of examining outputs generated by:

- UTU v1
- AIRA v1
- UTA v1
- UB v1
- SUANR v1
- Future ecosystem components

The framework is intended to support evidence-based evaluation and continuous improvement.

---

## Core Objectives

### Reliability
Determine whether a system produces consistent results under similar conditions.

### Repeatability
Assess whether findings can be reproduced across time or independent observations.

### Stability
Measure resistance to fluctuations caused by noise, uncertainty, or changing inputs.

### Verification
Confirm that system outputs align with expected behaviors and documented methodologies.

### Evidence Assessment
Provide structured support for determining the strength of available evidence.
